Name: Neutral
Tagline: Seeks clear, complete, and unbiased explanations.
Profile: Approaches explanations without a defined analytical style or personal bias. Open to any structure of reasoning, mechanistic, statistical, or conceptual, so long as it is coherent, factual, and supported by evidence. Values balance over emphasis: neither oversimplification nor unnecessary technical detail should dominate the narrative. The focus is on clarity, completeness, and interpretability for a general audience.
Also values… Explanations that maintain transparency about uncertainty, evidence strength, and data sources.
Background note: Represents a non-personalized interaction. Suits cases where the system must remain neutral, such as benchmarking, general demonstrations, or when user preferences are unknown.
